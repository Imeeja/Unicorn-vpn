What exactly is a VPN? +

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) creates a more private connection between your device and the internet, helping protect your online activity and masking your IP address.

Can I use Unicorn on multiple devices? +

Absolutely! A VPN can be used across your devices. The exact device limit would depend on the plan you choose.

Will a VPN slow down my internet? +

Any VPN may affect connection speed, but a well-optimized network aims to keep your browsing smooth and fast.

How do I get started? +

Choose your plan, set up the app on your device, connect to a location, and start exploring.
